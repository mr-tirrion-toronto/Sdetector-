How to read this dashboard

Risk categories reflect this scanner's configured signals and evidence. They are not a calibrated probability of fraud. A low score or missing threat-feed match does not prove a website is safe.

This dashboard shows statistics for your signed-in account only. Threat-feed health is shared platform status; other accounts’ scans, submitted URLs and reports are not shown.

Evidence boundary: dashboard figures summarize scans recorded by Scam Detector. They do not represent all websites on the internet or prove that any website is safe.
