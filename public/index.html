Important security note
Scam Detector combines URL-pattern analysis, live DNS lookups, Certificate Transparency history, RDAP registration data, public website-content inspection, PhishDestroy domain intelligence and local threat-feed matching when feeds are current. Firecrawl receives only the public hostname and path; URL query strings, fragments and browser credentials are removed. Hostnames with private or reserved DNS answers are not sent to Firecrawl. Certificate Transparency is not a live TLS handshake, and redirect reporting is limited to the final URL reported by Firecrawl. This is not a full malware sandbox and it does not execute downloaded files. A zero score or missing feed match is not proof that a website is safe. Public-page content cannot independently prove that a platform pays its users. Business identity and payout claims require independent verification. Never submit passwords, payment details, one-time codes or private information to a site you do not trust.
